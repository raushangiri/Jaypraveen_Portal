# Political Leader Digital Engagement Portal

## Business Requirement & Scope Document

### 1. Project Overview

The objective is to create a modern, multilingual political engagement platform for a political leader/public figure that serves as a central hub for:

* Citizen engagement
* Volunteer enrollment
* Supporter management
* Social media aggregation
* Public communication
* Campaign outreach
* Opinion gathering
* Event participation
* Political intelligence

The portal should help the leader build a strong digital community while collecting meaningful insights from supporters and citizens.

---

# 2. Business Goals

### Primary Goals

1. Increase public reach and visibility.
2. Convert social media followers into active supporters.
3. Build a verified volunteer database.
4. Understand citizen concerns and opinions.
5. Promote political ideology and initiatives.
6. Improve communication with citizens.
7. Increase participation in campaigns and events.
8. Build constituency-level engagement.

### Success Metrics

* Number of registrations
* Number of volunteers
* Social media engagement
* Event participation
* Survey responses
* Newsletter subscribers
* Constituency-wise supporter growth
